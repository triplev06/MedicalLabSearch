> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task by task; use parallel research for independent regions.

**Goal:** Add exactly 100 distinct source-backed laboratories beyond the existing 89 entries, prioritizing established biomedical research institutions in major employment metros.

**Architecture:** Three independently researched typed modules feed the existing catalog. Public lab data stays separate from browser-private applications. Geographic filters derive represented states from a complete state-name map; discovery renders results in accessible batches to keep the larger catalog responsive.

**Tech Stack:** Existing React/TypeScript/Vite/Dexie stack; no additional dependencies or services.

**Spec:** Existing Research Atlas design and the user's request for another 100 labs nationwide, in good standing and good economic areas, preferably major cities.

## Constraints and review focus

- Exactly 100 new distinct PI groups; preserve all 89 existing IDs and private tracking records.
- Official lab or detailed faculty sources for every new group. A research page is not proof of hiring.
- Scientific alignment uses the existing minimized qualifications; no private resume information is sent externally.
- Five-point fit/prestige bands with evidence-based lab-specific explanations; no acceptance probabilities.
- Interpret economic-area preference as established biomedical employment ecosystems, not guaranteed growth, affordability, lab funding or personal safety. Document primary market research and its date.
- Handle newly represented states, exact city plus query combinations, batch expansion and filter reset without concealing matches.
