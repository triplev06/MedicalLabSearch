# Nationwide laboratory and location selection

Reviewed October 21, 2026. This expansion adds exactly 100 PI-led laboratory groups to the existing 89 catalog entries. All individual records include official lab or detailed faculty research sources, scientific fit explanations, skill gaps and explicit vacancy/eligibility caveats. Institutional research directories identify candidates; they do not substitute for group-level sources or prove hiring.

## Meaning of the location preference

“Good economic areas” is interpreted as established or developing biomedical employment ecosystems: multiple research/healthcare institutions, relevant life-sciences talent and potential alternative employers in the wider metro. Selection favors major metros rather than small isolated college towns. Berkeley and Pasadena are included within the Bay Area and Los Angeles metros; Aurora within the Denver area; Cambridge within Greater Boston; Durham within the Research Triangle.

This is a geographic selection rationale, not a new numerical economic score. It does not establish an individual lab's funding, salary, benefits, cost of living, neighborhood conditions or personal employment eligibility. Those require a specific role and relocation budget. Research strength and employment availability remain separate in the app.

## Source basis

- [CBRE: U.S. Life Sciences Talent Trends 2025](https://www.cbre.com/insights/reports/us-life-sciences-talent-trends-2025), June 2025, provides a consistent cross-market framework based on relevant jobs, graduates and labor costs. It identifies major coastal and inland life-sciences clusters, discusses Minneapolis–St. Paul's medtech strength, and describes St. Louis and Nashville as university-supported emerging-talent markets. It is market research, not a personal affordability assessment.
- [CBRE: Six Life Sciences Talent Trends Driving Property Demand](https://www.cbre.com/insights/briefs/6-life-sciences-talent-trends-driving-property-demand), 2026, updates the employment context. It discusses established hubs, secondary-market employment and emerging Dallas–Fort Worth/Pittsburgh investment. This newer context supplements the 2025 cross-market comparison; no market is assumed to have guaranteed future growth.
- [Metro Atlanta Chamber: Why Metro Atlanta Is a Global Life Sciences Hub](https://metroatlantachamber.com/why-metro-atlanta-is-a-global-life-sciences-hub/), August 28, 2026, documents Atlanta's university, healthcare, federal research and industry ecosystem. This is a local economic-development source with a promotional perspective; institutional names and infrastructure inform selection, not its marketing superlatives.
- [Tampa Bay EDC: Tampa Medical & Research District](https://tampabayedc.com/investor/tampa-medical-research-district/) documents the local academic-healthcare and research ecosystem. It is a local development source, not independent evidence of a particular lab vacancy or living costs.
- [NCI: Find an NCI-Designated Cancer Center](https://www.cancer.gov/research/infrastructure/cancer-centers/find) offers an independent official reference for participating cancer institutions and locations. Designation is not claimed for every university/group in the dataset and is not proof of employment availability.

## Availability and limits

The additions include one explicit research-technician advertisement (Ventura/MSK), three inquiry invitations (Fuchs/Rockefeller, Oliver/Duke and James Chen/UT Southwestern), 95 research-interest targets and one explicit closed route (Stanger/Penn). Deadlines remain unknown; no future deadline was fabricated. Graduate/postdoctoral instructions are kept distinct from assistant employment. A source's explicit invitation can support an inquiry label without establishing a funded position.

All 89 existing IDs are retained so browser-local tracking and prior backups remain compatible. The public modules contain only institutional information and minimized scientific qualification categories. Contacting labs, sending applications, checking pay and evaluating a specific relocation remain user actions.
